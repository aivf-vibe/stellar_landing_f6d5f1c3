Our Story

Brewing tradition meets modern innovation

Passion for Craft

Founded with a deep love for brewing, Higs Bar represents the perfect blend of traditional techniques and innovative flavors. Our master brewers carefully select the finest hops, malts, and yeast to create beers that tell a story in every sip.

From our humble beginnings to becoming a cornerstone of the local craft beer scene, we've remained committed to quality, community, and the art of brewing exceptional beer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Higs Bar

Do you offer brewery tours?

Yes! We offer guided brewery tours every Saturday at 2 PM and 4 PM. Tours include a behind-the-scenes look at our brewing process and a tasting of four signature beers. Reservations recommended.

Can I buy beer to take home?

Absolutely! We offer growler fills, crowlers, and packaged beer for takeaway. Our retail selection includes all our core beers plus seasonal and limited releases.

Do you serve food?

We partner with local food trucks and restaurants to provide delicious food options that pair perfectly with our beers. Check our events calendar for current food offerings.

Is the taproom family-friendly?

Yes! Families are welcome until 8 PM. We have non-alcoholic beverages and a kid-friendly atmosphere during earlier hours.
